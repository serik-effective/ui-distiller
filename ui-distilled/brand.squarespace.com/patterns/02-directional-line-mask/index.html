Directional line mask

typography · motion — trigger: swap the text, or replay

Lines leave the way they came in, not the way they arrived from.

A masked line usually enters from below and, on exit, retreats back down — which reads as a rewind. Here the line continues past the top edge instead, so replacing one block of text with another is a single upward movement handed from the outgoing line to the incoming one.
