Pinned poster sequence

scroll · 3d · typography — trigger: scroll through the pinned section

Two animations sharing one scroll range, in sequence.

The section is five viewports tall and its stage is sticky, so scrolling moves a progress value rather than the page. That value is split into stages: the caption converges from both edges and fades up, the composition holds still for a moment, then the poster rotates away in 3D. Every property is computed from progress, so scrolling backwards is exact.
