Elastic pulse

microinteraction · buttons — trigger: hover any control

The squash is measured in ems, not pixels.

On hover the control widens by three quarters of its own font size and loses a third of that in height, then springs back on an elastic curve. Because the deformation is typographic rather than proportional, a 12px pill and a 34px one distort by the same visual amount — a percentage scale would make the big one look rubbery and the small one look inert.
