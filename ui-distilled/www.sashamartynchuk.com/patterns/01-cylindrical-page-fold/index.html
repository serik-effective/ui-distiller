Cylindrical page fold

scroll · 3d · layout — trigger: scroll; rows fold over the bottom edge

Rows wrap over the bottom edge instead of sliding under it.

Each row is mapped onto a cylinder as it approaches the fold line: it curves through the arc, then carries on along the tangent. Because the container's vanishing point follows the scroll, the fold stays directly in front of the reader wherever they are on the page.
